About Our College

Turath College of Education, Kano is a premier institution dedicated to the training of future teachers. Our name, "Turath" (Heritage), reflects our commitment to preserving and integrating Islamic knowledge and values into modern educational practices.

We offer fully accredited NCE (Nigeria Certificate in Education) programs designed to equip students with the pedagogical skills, subject knowledge, and ethical foundation required to excel in today's classrooms.

Our serene campus provides the perfect environment for learning, research, and character development.

Our NCE Programs

NCE in Arabic & Islamic Studies

Designed for students passionate about teaching Islamic education and the Arabic language in primary and junior secondary schools.

NCE in Primary Education Studies

A comprehensive program that prepares teachers to handle all subjects in primary schools with modern pedagogical techniques.

NCE in English Education

Equipping future educators with the skills to teach English language and literature effectively.

NCE in Mathematics Education

Develop the analytical skills needed to teach mathematics and make it accessible and engaging for students.

NCE in Computer Science

Preparing teachers for the digital age, focusing on IT literacy and computer science education.

NCE in Integrated Science

Training educators to teach foundational science subjects (Biology, Chemistry, Physics) at the basic education level.

Admissions & Requirements

Entry Requirements for NCE

  • Five (5) O'Level credit passes (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB) including English Language and Mathematics.
  • Passes must be in relevant subjects for your chosen program.
  • Candidates must participate in the JAMB UTME examination (for JAMB candidates) or apply directly for Direct Entry.
  • Must possess good character and be of sound mind.
